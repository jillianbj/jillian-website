About Me

Hello! I am a software engineer with over 8 years of experience building scalable backend services, cloud-native platforms, and developer tooling. I focus on creating reliable, maintainable software with emphasis on performance, observability, and straightforward architecture.

Throughout my career at companies like CloudScale Technologies and Apex Data Systems, I have architected multi-tenant Kubernetes platforms hosting hundreds of microservices, engineered high-throughput Kafka telemetry pipelines processing over 1.2 million events per second, and designed internal developer CLIs adopted across 15 engineering squads.

I hold a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Washington (Cum Laude) and am a Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) and AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional. Outside of backend systems and distributed computing, I enjoy cycling, open-source software, and mechanical keyboards.
